SUZUKI CULTUS 2004 MANUAL CLUSTER

Analog Needle Meter & Basic Indicators

Your 2004 Suzuki Cultus uses a direct analog temperature gauge needle alongside basic Oil Pressure (Red Can), Battery Charge, and Handbrake indicators. Always monitor the C-to-H temperature needle during city traffic and inspect radiator water manually before long drives.

✨ 1. Double-Layer Coating & Formula 1 Carnauba Wax SOP

1) Wash thoroughly with Flamingo Car Shampoo (Two-Bucket Method, Top-to-Bottom). 2) Spray Flamingo Nano Crystal Coating (F112) on cool body & buff with clean microfiber. 3) Allow 24-hour curing gap. 4) Wipe surface dust & apply Formula 1 Carnauba Wax for deep gloss protection.

🪟 2. DIY Glass Cleaner & Bug Splatter Rule

Mix 2 to 3 drops of dishwash in 1 Litre clean water. Always spray onto the microfiber cloth (never directly on painted edges) so your wax coat remains intact. Soften bug splatters with shampoo foam instead of scraping with fingernails.

🔋 3. Battery Water, Radiator & 15-Day Checkups

Inspect battery distilled water level, radiator coolant reservoir, and tyre air pressure (32 PSI) every 15 days. After applying Flamingo Tire Foam, wipe excess foam with a damp cloth for a natural black finish.
